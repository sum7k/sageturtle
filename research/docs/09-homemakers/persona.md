# Kavita Sharma, 36 (Homemakers)

Fictional. The numbers are real and listed in `../sources.md`.

## Who she is
- Lives in a two-room flat in Gomti Nagar Extension, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, with her husband, two children (12 and
  8) and her mother-in-law, who has arthritis.
- BA in Hindi. Worked for a year in a school office before marriage.
- Her husband is an accounts clerk on Rs 32,000 a month. The household money is his. She asks for what she needs.
- Known in the building for her achar and for stitching blouses.

## Her day
Up at 5.30. Tiffins, school bus, cooking, washing, her mother-in-law's medicines and bath, homework, dinner. She
has two free windows: 11 to 1 and 3 to 4.30. They go to the phone or to sleep.

## What is hard
- Her whole day is unpaid work. Women spend 289 minutes a day on housework against 88 for men, and 137 minutes on
  caring for family against 75 (Time Use Survey 2024, as reported by ORF).
- Only 25% of women aged 15 to 59 do any paid work, against 75% of men (Time Use Survey 2024).
- She has no money that is her own, and feels it each time she asks.
- A job outside is not possible. Nobody else can do the 5.30 to 10 day.
- She has skills people already praise, but selling to strangers feels unsafe and "not done".

## Her biggest problem
She has skill and two free hours but no way to turn them into money of her own without leaving home.

## The app
**Haath.** Neighbours within a short walk can order what she makes or does: achar, tiffin, stitching. Orders fit
the hours she sets. See `case.md`.
