## What the 11 have in common
- **One fact, spoken, at the right moment.** Parakh, Kavach, Hisaab, Bhav and Dawa each give the person one true
  thing they cannot get today: is it real, how long am I safe, what did I keep, what is the price, did I take it.
- **Proof and paths for the young.** Raah and Namuna are about seeing work and showing work, not about learning
  content.
- **A patient helper where a person is missing.** Kahani stands in for a listener, Toli for four more teachers.
- **Money of one's own, close to home.** Bahi and Haath are small trade between neighbours.
- None of the 11 asked for a feed, a chat or a social network.

## Strongest and weakest
- Strongest evidence: Parakh (93% have met fake offers), Kahani (23.4% reading), Toli (1,04,125 single-teacher
  schools), Bhav (surplus of Rs 1,951 a month).
- Weakest: Bahi (others already do it, and no checked figure), Namuna (needs employers to give time).
- Cheapest to test this week with no screens: Bhav, Hisaab, Dawa, Haath.
