# Namuna: product case

For Sneha, 20, in `persona.md`.

## 1. The problem
"I will have a BA and nothing else. When they ask what I have done, I have no answer. Nobody gives a first chance
to someone with no work to show."

## 2. Jobs to be done
- Give me a small real task from a real employer that I can finish in a week beside college.
- Have that employer tell me honestly what was good and what was weak.
- Keep my finished work in one place I can show at an interview.

## 3. What it is not for
- Not courses, certificates or tests.
- Not a job board. A task may lead to a job, but that is not promised.
- Not free labour. A task is small, a few hours, and the employer must give feedback or it does not count.

## 4. The number
**Students with one piece of work reviewed by an employer within 30 days of joining.** Target: 1 in 3.

Guardrails that must not get worse:
- No task takes more than 6 hours. No employer gets more than 3 unpaid tasks done in a month.
- Every submitted task gets feedback within 7 days.
- A student's work is hers. The employer may not use it without paying.

## 5. The riskiest assumption
Local employers will take the trouble to set a task and review a stranger's work. Students are easy to find.
Employers are the scarce side.

## 6. Test with 5 real students
- Who: 5 students in BA, BCom or BSc with no work experience. sumeet recruits them, and also asks 2 local business
  owners he knows to each set one real task.
- How: clickable screens showing the 2 tasks. The work itself is done for real, over one week.
- Pass: 4 of 5 students submit, and both employers give feedback on every submission within 7 days without being
  chased more than once.
- If it fails on the student side: tasks are too large or too vague, so shrink them. If it fails on the employer
  side: stop, and test whether college teachers or alumni will review instead.
