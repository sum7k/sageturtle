# Sources

Looked up on 2026-11-28. "Secondary" means the number was read in a news or think-tank report of the survey, not
in the survey itself.

  reading 44.8%; Class 5 division 30.7%.
  https://asercentre.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/ASER-2024-National-findings.pdf
- LocalCircles, Aug to Oct 2024, urban parents of 9 to 17 year olds. 66% say the child is addicted; 47% say 3 or
  more hours a day. Secondary.
  https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/more-than-50-of-parents-believe-children-addicted-to-social-media-gaming-are-getting-aggressive-impatient-and-lethargic-says-survey-452670-2024-11-05
- UDISE+ 2024-25. 1,04,125 single-teacher schools, 33,76,769 students; Jharkhand 4,36,480. Secondary.
  https://theprint.in/india/education/over-33-lakh-indian-students-enrolled-in-schools-with-just-one-teacher-issue-most-stark-in-up-jharkhand/2733622
- Teachers' paperwork, accounts only, no national figure.
  https://theprint.in/the-fineprint/govt-schoolteachers-drowning-classwork-homework-more-work/2304481

## Teens

  https://www.freepressjournal.in/education/aser-2023-report-india-youth-lacks-career-clarity-no-role-model-in-life
  https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-editorials/aser-2023-examining-education-beyond-basics/print_manually

## College students and job seekers
- India Skills Report 2025 (Wheebox). Graduate employability 54.81%. Secondary.
  https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/employability-among-indian-graduates-improves-to-54-81-wheebox-report-124121001152_1.html
- NITI Aayog, "Reimagining Skilling for Viksit Bharat", reported 27 Aug 2026. 8.25% of graduates in jobs matching
  their degree; 3.4 crore undergraduates, 1.13 crore in BA; Rs 22,000 against Rs 29,000 starting pay. Secondary.
  https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/only-8-25-graduates-in-jobs-matching-their-degrees-niti-aayog-report-flags-skills-gap-551623-2026-08-27

  Secondary.
  https://fortune.com/asia/2024/03/30/young-people-india-more-likely-unemployed-if-educated-international-labour-organization
- Indeed India, 1,161 workers, reported 9 July 2026. 93% met fake offers; 51% cannot tell; 75% ignore offers; 14%
  missed real ones; 46% of Gen Z lost money. Secondary.
  https://www.businesstoday.in/jobs/story/more-than-9-in-10-job-seekers-encounter-fake-job-offers-542006-2026-07-09

  31% have both an emergency fund and enough insurance. Secondary.
  https://businesstoday.in/jobs/story/when-the-paycheck-stops-would-your-finances-survive-a-job-loss-546687-2026-08-02
- Tata AIG, Corporate Health Protection Pulse 2026, 748 employees. Nearly 7 in 10 last six months or less; 45% rely
  only on employer cover; about 75% say it is not enough. Secondary.
  https://businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/rising-healthcare-costs-emerge-as-top-worry-for-india-inc-employees-tata-aig-survey-548309-2026-08-10

  and household costs.
  https://www.grantthornton.in/insights/thought-leadership/indias-financial-resilience-report-2026/

## Seniors
- HelpAge India Report 2024, 5,169 elders in 20 cities. 54% two or more illnesses; 48% blood pressure; 43%
  diabetes; 31% insured; 65% not financially secure; 52% difficulty with daily activities; 59% no digital device;
  13% of users bank or pay online.
  https://helpageindia.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/NATIONAL-SALIENT-FINDINGS-HelpAge-India-Report-2024.pdf
- ThePrint, 9 June 2025. Elders targeted in 45 of 60 digital arrest cases with Delhi's cyber unit; cybercrime
  against elders up 86% from 2020 (NCRB 2022). Secondary.
  https://theprint.in/feature/india-elderly-losing-savings-scams-health-problems/2651557
