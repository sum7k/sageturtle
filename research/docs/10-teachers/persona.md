# Sunita Kumari, 38 (Teachers)

Fictional. The numbers are real and listed in `../sources.md`.

## Who she is
- The only teacher at a government primary school in a village in Latehar district, Jharkhand. 62 children in
  Classes 1 to 5, in two rooms.
- BA and a diploma in elementary education. Twelve years in service. Lives in the block town 14 km away and comes
  by scooter.
- The children speak Kurukh and Sadri at home. The books are in Hindi.
- She is also the head, the clerk and the one who signs for the mid-day meal.

## Her day
At 9 she has five classes and one voice. She puts Classes 4 and 5 to copying, gives Class 3 sums on the board, and
sits with Classes 1 and 2. By 10.30 the older children are restless and she has not got back to them. After lunch
she fills in attendance, the meal count and the school portal on her phone, on a weak signal.

## What is hard
- She is alone. India has 1,04,125 single-teacher schools with 33,76,769 children in them. Jharkhand has 4,36,480
  of those children, second only to Uttar Pradesh (UDISE+ 2024-25).
- Within one class the children are at four levels. In rural government schools only 23.4% of Class 3 children can
  read a Class 2 text, and 44.8% of Class 5 (ASER 2024).
- Her training and the textbooks assume one teacher for one class.
- Paperwork and portals take the hour she could plan in. We found no national figure for this time, only teachers'
  own accounts (ThePrint, October 2024).
- While she teaches one group, the other four learn nothing.

## Her biggest problem
Five classes, one teacher. For most of the day, most of her children have nothing to do that is at their level.

## The app
**Toli.** Each morning it gives her a plan for the day: the children in groups by what they can do, and for each
group a task they can run themselves while she teaches another. See `case.md`.
