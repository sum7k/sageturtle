# Toli: product case

For Sunita, 38, in `persona.md`.

## 1. The problem
"When I sit with the small ones, the big ones make noise. When I go to the big ones, the small ones run out. I am
one person. The book does not tell me what to do with five classes."

## 2. Jobs to be done
- Tell me in the morning which children sit together today, by what they can do and not by class.
- For each group, give me a task they can do on their own for 20 minutes with what is in my school.
- Tell me which group needs me first, and when to move.

## 3. What it is not for
- Not for the children to use. One phone, in the teacher's hand.
- Not attendance, the meal count or any report to the department.
- Not a new syllabus. It uses her textbooks, slates, stones and the floor.

## 4. The number
**School days in a week on which the teacher ran the plan.** She says so by voice at day's end. Target: 3 of 5.

Guardrails that must not get worse:
- The morning plan is heard in under 3 minutes.
- No task needs anything the school does not have.
- Nothing she says goes to her officers.

## 5. The riskiest assumption
A group of young children will stay on a task for 20 minutes with no adult. If they will not, no plan helps.
Second risk: she will trust a phone's grouping over her own habit of teaching by class.

## 6. Test with 5 real teachers
- Who: 5 teachers who teach two or more classes at once. sumeet recruits them.
- How: clickable screens with one fixed day's plan for 3 groups, using tasks from known multigrade and Teaching at
  the Right Level practice. Each teacher runs it for 3 school days.
- Pass: 4 of 5 run it on at least 2 of 3 days, and say the groups stayed on task for 15 minutes or more.
- If it fails: if the children did not stay on task, shorten tasks to 10 minutes and add a child leader for each
  group. If teachers did not start, the plan is too far from what they do now.
