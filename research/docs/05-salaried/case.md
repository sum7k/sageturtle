# Kavach: product case

For Priya, 31, in `persona.md`.

## 1. The problem
"If my job goes or Achan falls ill again, I do not know how many months we can stand. Everyone who gives me advice
wants to sell me a policy."

## 2. Jobs to be done
- Tell me, in months, how long my family is safe if my salary stops.
- Tell me the one step to take this month, and why that one first.
- Tell me when a step is done, and what it added.

## 3. What it is not for
- It sells nothing and takes no commission from any bank, insurer or fund.
- Not an expense tracker. It asks for 6 numbers once, not every purchase.
- Not investment advice or stock tips.

## 4. The number
**Users who finish one step in a month.** Target: 1 in 3. The safe months of the family should rise with it.

Guardrails that must not get worse:
- No product is named for payment, ever.
- The 6 numbers stay on the phone.
- Nobody is told to do something that leaves less than one month of cash in hand.

## 5. The riskiest assumption
A person will act on a step from an app with no human adviser and no reward, when the step costs money or effort
today. Second risk: people will say their real numbers.

## 6. Test with 5 real salaried people
- Who: 5 salaried people aged 25 to 40 with at least one EMI. sumeet recruits them.
- How: clickable screens. Each person enters their own 6 numbers and sees their months and one step.
- Do: ask them to say out loud what they will do. Call back after 3 weeks.
- Pass: 5 of 5 can repeat their months figure and step, and 2 of 5 have done the step after 3 weeks.
- If it fails: if they understood but did not act, test the step with a named friend as witness. If they did not
  trust the figure, show how it was worked out.
