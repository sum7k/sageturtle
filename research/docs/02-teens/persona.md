# Arjun Meena, 16 (Teens 13 to 17)

Fictional. The numbers are real and listed in `../sources.md`.

## Who he is
- Class 11, Arts, in the government senior secondary school of a village in Dausa district, Rajasthan.
- Father farms 3 bighas and drives a tractor on hire. Mother works on the farm. An elder sister is married.
- He took Arts because the school has no science teacher.
- He has his own second-hand smartphone since Class 10.

## His day
School until 1. He helps with the cattle, then cricket, then the phone: reels, cricket clips and a battle game until
midnight. He tells everyone he will "do a government job" but cannot say which one.

## What is hard
- He does not know what kinds of work exist. 1 in 5 rural youth aged 14 to 18 cannot name any job they want
  (ASER 2023).
- He knows nobody who does a job he could copy. 42.5% of boys and 48.3% of girls have no role model for the work
  they hope for (ASER 2023).
- 55.7% of rural students in Class 11 and above are in Arts, like him (ASER 2023), and nobody tells them what Arts
  leads to.
- About 80% of rural youth use the phone for entertainment (ASER 2023). His is for almost nothing else.
- Only 43.3% of 14 to 18 year olds can do a simple division sum (ASER 2023). He is not one of them.

## His biggest problem
He has two years to choose a path and has never seen one. The only paths he knows are farming, the army and "a
government job".

## The app
**Raah.** Short spoken stories from people a few years older, from villages like his, about the work they do and the
first step they took. See `case.md`.
