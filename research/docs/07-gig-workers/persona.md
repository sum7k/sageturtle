# Suresh Kumar, 29 (Gig and daily-wage workers)

Fictional. The numbers are real and listed in `../sources.md`.

## Who he is
- Food delivery rider in Hyderabad, on two apps. From a village in Nalgonda district, Telangana. Studied to Class
  10.
- His wife and 4 year old son live in the village with his parents. He shares a room with three other riders for
  Rs 2,500 a month.
- The bike is on a loan, EMI Rs 3,400. Petrol is about Rs 300 a day.
- When orders are thin he takes loading work at a godown for Rs 600 a day.

## His day
On the road from 11 in the morning to midnight, with a break at 4. The app shows "Rs 1,150 earned today". After
petrol, a puncture, a Rs 120 cut he does not understand and food, he cannot say what is left. Once a month he sends
home what remains, and it is never what he expected.

## What is hard
- Long hours: about 85% of platform workers work more than 8 hours a day (Janpahal survey of 5,220 workers, 2024).
- Low and uneven pay: 40% of India's roughly 12 million gig workers earn under Rs 15,000 a month (Economic Survey,
  January 2026).
- The app's "earnings" is before his costs. Nobody shows him the real figure.
- Cuts and penalties arrive with no reason given, and an account can be switched off without warning.
- With no steady figure he cannot plan the EMI, the room rent and the money for home.

## His biggest problem
He does not know what he really earns. So he cannot tell which hours, which app or which days are worth his body.

## The app
**Hisaab.** Each night it tells him in one sentence what he kept today after petrol and cuts, and on Sunday which
hours paid best. See `case.md`.
