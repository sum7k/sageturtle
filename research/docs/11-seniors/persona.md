# Gopalrao Deshpande, 71 (Retired and seniors)

Fictional. The numbers are real and listed in `../sources.md`.

## Who he is
- Retired clerk of the state transport corporation. Lives in his own small house in Nagpur, Maharashtra, with his
  wife Sudha, 66.
- Pension is Rs 19,000 a month. Their son is an engineer in Bengaluru and calls on Sundays. Their daughter is in
  Nashik.
- Has high blood pressure and diabetes. Sudha has thyroid trouble and a bad knee.
- Got a smartphone from his son two years ago. Uses it for calls, WhatsApp and bhajans. His eyes tire quickly.

## His day
A walk at 6, the newspaper, the temple, the bank on pension day. Between them the two take 9 tablets a day at 4
different times. The strips look alike. He misses the afternoon one about twice a week, and last month took the
blood pressure tablet twice in one morning and fell dizzy. The doctor changes doses every three months and he keeps
the slips in a biscuit tin. His son asks "are you taking your medicines?" and he says yes.

## What is hard
- He has two long illnesses, like most. 54% of elders have two or more, 48% have high blood pressure and 43% have
  diabetes (HelpAge India 2024, 5,169 elders in 20 cities).
- 52% of elders have difficulty with at least one daily activity (HelpAge India 2024).
- Only 31% have health insurance, and 65% do not feel financially secure (HelpAge India 2024). A hospital stay
  would eat his savings.
- 59% of elders have no digital device at all. Of those who have one, only 13% use it to pay or bank (HelpAge India
  2024).
- He fears the phone. Elders were the target in 45 of 60 open "digital arrest" cases with Delhi's cyber unit, and
  cybercrime against elders rose 86% from 2020 to 2022 (ThePrint, June 2025). This is his second biggest problem.

## His biggest problem
He cannot keep 9 tablets a day straight, and one mistake can put him in a hospital he cannot afford. His children
are far away and do not know.

## The app
**Dawa.** At each time it says aloud which tablet to take and shows its picture. If one is missed, his son hears.
See `case.md`.
