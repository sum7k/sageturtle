# Research: apps from personas

Asked by sumeet on 2026-11-28.

A thought experiment. Forget the current app list. Write one fictional but realistic Indian person for each of
BodhiLabs's 11 variants, ground their daily troubles in real Indian data, and ask: what one app would fix this
person's biggest problem? Then give that app a one-page case in the format of SDLC step 1.

The point is to see which apps the people themselves call for, and to compare that with the apps we already plan.

- Start at `docs/summary.md`.
- Each variant has a folder in `docs/` with `persona.md` and `case.md`.
- Every number is in `docs/sources.md`.
- The personas are made up. The 5 real people in each test are different, and sumeet recruits them.
