# Research tradeoffs

Format: `- [where] Choice. Why. Cost.` All choices below are Claude's, made on 2026-11-28.

- [method] The current app list, idea files and designs were not opened. Why: sumeet asked for apps arrived at
  independently. Cost: the project memory note on variants names the 11 planned apps in one line, so Claude knew
  the names, though not what each does.
- [personas] One person per variant, across 6 states, 6 male and 5 female, mostly small town and rural.
  Why: spread, and most of India is not metro. Cost: one person cannot stand for a whole variant, and the south, east and north-east are thin.
- [personas] The biggest problem was picked by what the data shows is common and what one app could move. Why: an
  app cannot fix low pay or missing teachers. Cost: larger problems are named in the persona and left alone.
- [apps] One-word noun names in Hindi: Kahani, Raah, Namuna, Parakh, Kavach, Bahi, Hisaab, Bhav, Haath, Toli, Dawa.
  Why: the standing name rule. Cost: working names only, not checked for trademarks.
- [cases] "Jobs to be done" lists the person's jobs, not one line per variant. Why: each app here is for one
  variant. Cost: differs slightly from the SDLC case format.
- [tests] 4 of the 11 tests (Hisaab, Bhav, Haath, Dawa) need no screens at all, only voice notes, cards or alarms run by hand. Why: fastest way
  to test the riskiest assumption. Cost: the SDLC says the test runs on clickable screens.
- [seniors] Medicines chosen over fraud protection. Why: it is daily, and the scam check is already the job seeker
  app. Cost: fraud is a close second and is not covered.
- [shopkeepers] Kept the credit ledger though Khatabook and OkCredit exist. Why: it is still his biggest problem.
  Cost: the weakest case of the 11, and no checked figure for kirana credit was found.
- [data] Several numbers come from news reports of a survey, not the survey itself. Why: time. Cost: marked
  "secondary" in `docs/sources.md`.
- [repo] Not put in the repository. Why: the device folder is not a git checkout and nobody asked for it there.
  Cost: none yet. If wanted, it goes on dev.
