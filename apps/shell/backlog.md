# Shell: backlog

Things parked on purpose. Each line says what it is and what it needs before work can start. Newest at the bottom.

- **A child on their own phone.** How a child's profile is put on a second phone, and how the owner approves a change from their own phone. Needs: the Me app's design.
- **Getting an account back.** What a person does when the phone is lost and its passkeys were not backed up. Needs: a choice between a phone number, a family member's approval, or nothing.
- **Drawn pictures.** The nine "what you do" pictures and the app tile pictures in Khabri's sun-and-hill style. Needs: the flow agreed first.
- **Price of voice.** The cost of speech in and speech out in 12 languages for the speak button and the Listen pill. Needs: a count of expected use and a vendor comparison.
- **Desktop.** What the landing page and the app do on a computer. Needs: a decision on whether desktop install is allowed.
- **Check the install pictures on real phones.** Where each browser puts its install or share control, for Chrome, Samsung Internet, Firefox, Safari on iOS 18 and iOS 26, and the in-app browsers of WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook. Needs: the phones, or a device testing service.
- **Night colours for the other screens.** Only Home is drawn at night. Needs: your reaction to the night Home.
- **A child growing from Kids to Teens.** Add a child takes no birth date, so the move is by hand. Needs: a decision on asking the birth year.
