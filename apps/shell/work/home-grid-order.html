Cheiro's logic

  • Nine numbers, nine planets. 1 Sun, 2 Moon, 3 Jupiter, 4 Uranus, 5 Mercury, 6 Venus, 7 Neptune, 8 Saturn, 9 Mars.
  • Two linked pairs: 1 with 4 (Sun and Uranus) and 2 with 7 (Moon and Neptune). On the keypad 1 sits right above 4, so Earnings and Discussions touch.
  • *, 0 and # are not in his system, so Directory and Adverts can stay at the bottom freely.

Honest notes
