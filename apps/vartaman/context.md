# Vartaman: context (the bhav)

Read this before designing, writing or choosing anything for Vartaman. When a choice is unclear, decide by this.
(sumeet, 2026-11-28)

## Why this app exists

A person has learned to read and write. Now they have to live in this world: work, play, enjoy and express
themselves. For that they need to know what is going on. Vartaman brings **the knowledge of the present that matters
to a person who wants to do something in life**, in the things that interest them.

## Who it is for

Everyone. Gyan was made for children; Vartaman is for a child, an old person and everyone between, whatever their
work. Who the reader is makes no difference. Anyone can read any topic and understand it. That is why it is
arranged by topic and not by kind of reader.

## What is news here

- **What is moving is news.** What is going on and changing is news. What has gone stale and stopped changing
  leaves.
- **Only what matters.** News that has nothing to do with the reader's life is left out. A killing in a far country
  is not our news. A war between countries can be: it is a big thing and may touch the reader's trade and life.
- **No crime.** Petty crime and incidents are not our interest. There is no crime or courts topic.
- **The present, not history.** What has passed has passed.

## How it is written

- **Suitable for every age, always.** Children read this too. Plain language, with brutality, violence and
  sexuality taken out.
- **Never sensational.** Wrong things are not made exciting. When something terrible has happened, say calmly that
  something very bad happened there; do not dwell on deaths or detail.
- **Competition is reported as it is.** In Gyan there is no competition, because that is the time of learning. In
  the world a player does face an opponent, so sport and the like are told as they happen. Readers are still never
  compared with each other.
