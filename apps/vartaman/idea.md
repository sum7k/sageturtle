# Vartaman (Present, वर्तमान)

The second house on Home. News for every age, arranged by topic. Why it exists is in `context.md`.

## Shape

Two levels, each a 4x4 grid like the rest of the app.

- **Level 1: 12 topics.** These are fixed.
  देश (country), दुनिया (world), जगह (my state, town, village), व्यापार (business), खेती (farming, with weather),
  रोज़गार (work, with education and exams), खेल (sport), मनोरंजन (films, music, art), विज्ञान (science and tech),
  सेहत (health), समाज (society), प्रकृति (nature; the environment is part of it).
- **Level 2: 12 running stories in each topic.** The 12 houses are the stories themselves. They are not planned in
  advance, because nobody can know beforehand which news will matter.

## How the 12 stories change

1. News is fetched all the time, fast.
2. A new development that belongs to one of the 12 stories is added to that story.
3. A new story that passes the bhav (see `context.md`) takes the place of the story whose last update is the oldest:
   the one that has gone stale and stopped changing.
4. A story that is pushed out is gone. This is the present, not history. If it is ever worth studying, it will be
   taught in Gyan.
5. Nothing is added just because a day has passed. If nothing useful happened, the 12 stay as they are.

## Inside a story

- **Latest:** short snippets by time, newest on top.
- **Full story:** the whole thing from the start to now, written as one piece and made ahead of time.
