# Stories

Original stories for the CubeGullak learner: 12 for each of the 12 Gyan stages (pre-school plus classes 1 to 11), 144 in all.

- A stage's 12 stories open when the learner reaches that stage, so the tone and length fit the age.
- They are about dharma as religion based on logic: good values reached by clear reasoning, not by fear or command.
- Every story is a mind story: a problem nearly happens and someone thinks their way out. Happy twists, jumps,
  surprises and found things, in the playful spirit of Alice in Wonderland and Flatland, but all original.
- Non-sectarian. No gods, scriptures or religious figures appear. Suitable for children.
- sumeet reviews the themes; on 2026-11-28 he said to start writing in parallel, stage by stage.

Themes: docs/themes.md. Stories: docs/stage-01.md and so on, one file per stage.
