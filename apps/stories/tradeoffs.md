# Stories tradeoffs (choices Claude made, 2026-11-28)

- [themes] Each theme is tied to something the learner meets in that stage of Gyan (stage 6 has zero, stage 8 has Newton). Why: story and study strengthen each other. Cost: a theme may need to move if the syllabus map changes.
- [themes] "Dharma by logic" is read as: a value shown to follow from reasoning (fairness from "you cut, I choose", truth from accounts that must balance). Why: the uploaded framework file held only menu headings, so I worked from sumeet's message. Cost: the themes may not match his framework's own terms.
- [themes] No gods, scriptures, saints or named religions in any story. Why: non-sectarian. Cost: no familiar religious stories.
- [themes] No retellings of Panchatantra, Jataka, Aesop, Akbar-Birbal, Alice or Flatland. Why: sumeet asked for original stories. Cost: a few themes use well-known ideas (the shadow of a tree, the seven bridges, a finger entering a flat world) with new plots.
- [themes] Good is never rewarded by luck. The happy turn comes from someone's reasoning or its direct result. Why: the stories teach thinking. Cost: fewer pure magic endings.
- [stage 1, 2] Stories are heard with pictures, 2 to 3 minutes. Why: Gyan has no reading before stage 3. Cost: they need audio and art.
- [length] Half a page at stage 3, rising to five or six pages at stage 12. Cost: later stories take longer to write and translate.
- [stage 9 to 12] Some endings are mixed or open (the weaver's loss, the cheapest shirt, the last question). Why: honest for ages 13 to 16. Cost: not every twist is purely happy.
- [stage 6] "The Song and the Rain" tests the belief that a raga brings rain and finds it does not. Why: honest testing of a loved belief is the point. Cost: it may displease some; easy to drop.
- [characters] No fixed hero across stories; names and places are plain and Indian, spread across regions when written. A few later stories call back to earlier ones (10.8 and 11.5).
- [folder] The older fairytale extraction in docs/ (titles.md, stories/, img/) was left untouched and not used.
- [count] 12 stages x 12 themes = 144, by sumeet on 2026-11-28 (was 11 x 11). "The Question With No Answer Key" is kept as the very last story, 12.12.
- [stage 12] Themes use class 11 ideas (infinity, limits, chance, energy, the quantum world, relativity, the genetic code). Why: Gyan has no stage 12 map yet. Cost: may need changes once it does. Two endings are left open on purpose (12.6, 12.10).
- [themes] No competition, comparison or ranking inside the stories (sumeet, 2026-11-28). Nobody wins against anybody; the struggle is with the problem. 4.2, 5.9, 8.1, 11.11 and 12.2 were rewritten for this, and 1.9, 4.12, 5.4, 6.10 and 7.11 were reworded.
- [stage 1 stories] About 270 to 330 words each, short sentences, repeated lines a child can join in, a sound word where it fits. Named children, plain names. An adult is present where there is fire. In 1.8 the sweet lump is dried honeycomb, since the first people had no jaggery.
- [files] Stories are one file per stage (docs/stage-01.md), because docs/stories/ holds the old dropped extraction.
- [stage 2 stories] About 400 to 480 words each. 2.9 uses a count of drum beats (two for goats, three for boats, an echo adds one) instead of rhyming words, so that it works in all languages. In 2.9 the lucky result is called luck in the story, and the lesson is the repeat-back rule.
