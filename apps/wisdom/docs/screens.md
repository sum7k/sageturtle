## The quotations

- Opens from the Wisdom tile, top centre on Home.
- Top: a home button, the date, and the round picture that opens Me.

  that opens the story.
- Below: earlier quotations, one row each, with a small picture, the quotation and its source. A gold heart marks the
  ones the person kept. A tap opens the story.
- The only text is content: the quotation, its source and the date.

- Listen, share and a heart to keep it.
