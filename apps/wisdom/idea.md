Only the design exists. sumeet will talk through the details later, and the app is built last. The drawn screens are on
the shell canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/Jgem7AE3dhZSPvVkV6bqS1), and described in `docs/screens.md`.

## What it does

- Wisdom is the top centre tile on Home (position 2 of the keypad order, sumeet 2026-11-27). It has the same name in every variant.
- It holds a list of quotations. One new quotation comes each day.
- Each quotation is shown word for word, with its source.
- Under it is a short moral science story that says the same thing through an everyday life. The story ends in one line
  of moral.
- The story is different for each variant. The quotation and the moral stay the same.
- Everything can be heard. A heart keeps a quotation.
- No ads, no comments, no scores.

## The story for each variant

| Variant | The story is about |
| :--- | :--- |
| Kids (6 to 12) | A child's world of school, play and home. Three big pictures, heard first. No sharing |
| Teens (13 to 17) | Friends, exams, the phone, doing right when nobody is watching |
| College students | Hostel life, money from home, ambition, honesty in exams |
| Job seekers | Patience, rejection, effort without a result, refusing shortcuts |
| Salaried professionals | Duty at the office, credit and blame, anger, time for family |
| Business owners and shopkeepers | Fair weight, credit, keeping one's word, rivals in the market |
| Gig and daily-wage workers | Dignity in work, honest pay, helping a fellow worker. Short, heard more than read |
| Farmers and rural users | The field and the season: sharing water, debt, patience with a crop |
| Homemakers | The household, neighbours, thrift, work that nobody sees |
| Teachers | A teacher's duty, fairness between students. Fit to retell in class |
| Retired and senior citizens | Letting go, health, grandchildren, peace. Large print, read aloud |

A person with more than one variant gets one story per variant and can change between them.

## Scale

Nothing is written for one person. A quotation has 11 stories (one per variant) in 12 languages, so 132 texts and 132
audio files. They are made once, checked, and served as saved files. This holds for 100 crore users.
