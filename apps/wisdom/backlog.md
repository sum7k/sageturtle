# Wisdom: backlog

Things parked on purpose, for the talk with sumeet. Each line says what it is and what it needs. Newest at the bottom.

- **Which traditions are covered.** And in what balance. Needs: sumeet's choice.
- **Does a person choose.** Either everyone gets every tradition, or a person picks theirs in Me. The second means asking a person's religion, which is sensitive personal data. Needs: sumeet's choice.
- **What counts as a quotation.** Only scripture ("of god"), or also saints, teachers and proverbs. The name Wisdom allows a wider set. Needs: sumeet's choice.
- **Sources and rights.** Each quotation needs a text that is free to use, in the original and in translation. Root `tradeoffs.md` already has checked sources for the Gita (the `gita/gita` dataset, The Unlicense, and Besant's 1895 English) and for the Hitopadesha. Needs: a source list per tradition.
- **Who checks.** A person who can read the original must check each quotation, and someone must check each story. Root `tradeoffs.md` has an open human item to name a Sanskrit reader for Gita verses; the same person could check here. Needs: names.
- **Who writes the stories.** A model can draft the 11 stories and a person can check them, or people write them. Needs: a choice, and a cost for 132 texts and audio files per quotation.
- **Legal look.** Hurting religious feelings on purpose is a crime in India (section 299 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, earlier section 295A of the IPC). Needs: a rule for how a story may treat a faith, and a lawyer's look before launch.
- **No ads.** Drawn with no ads, like Khabri. This gives up income from the app. Needs: sumeet's yes.
- **The lamp picture.** An oil lamp stands for the app. Needs: sumeet's view on whether it fits every tradition.
- **The original language.** Whether the quotation is also shown and spoken in its original (Sanskrit, Arabic, Gurmukhi, Pali and others). Root `tradeoffs.md` notes that Sanskrit speech uses the Hindi voice and sounds imperfect. Needs: a choice, and a voice check.
- **Pictures for stories.** A kid's story needs three drawn pictures. Needs: a way to make them in Khabri's style at this number.
- **Screens not drawn.** The kept list, finding a quotation by voice, a daily alert, Teens and seven adult variants, night and no-network. Needs: the points above settled first.
- **Other Home drawings.** Home with a tile opened, Home while arranging, Home at night and Home with no network do not show the Wisdom tile yet. The landing page still shows 12 app pictures. Needs: a pass over the shell canvas.
