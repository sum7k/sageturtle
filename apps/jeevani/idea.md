# Jeevani (Lives, जीवनी)

The twelfth house on Home. Each tap opens the life of one person, a different one each time, to read and to hear in the learner's language.

- 144 lives: 12 groups of 12 (the 11 Gyan subjects plus one more group).
- No dream professions. Only lives: how the person learned, struggled and grew.
- Purpose: through a life a child sees why learning matters.
- The spirit: a life shows struggle, effort and values. It never says "be like this person", never ranks people and never compares the learner with anyone.
- Each life is 300 to 400 words, written first in simple English, translated later.
