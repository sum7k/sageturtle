BodhiLabs design map

13 apps, 11 kinds of user

Each app keeps one core job. The variant decides what content it leads with, how it speaks, what is hidden, and how the screen is laid out. Everything is voice first, and language and location then tune every variant.

What each app becomes

Pick an app to see it across all 11 variants, or pick a variant to see its 13 apps. Every variant has its own app names, shown in bold colour. They are working titles in English and would be localised.

    Variant profiles

    Tone, safety and UI belong mostly to the variant, so they are set once here and apply across all 13 apps.

    VariantToneSafety and permissionsUI

    Family account

    Decided: every account belongs to an adult. Kids and Teens exist only as profiles that the adult creates inside their own Me app. A child can never sign up alone.

    • OwnerAn adult, paying or not. Children are never asked to prove their age.
    • Child profilesThe owner creates each one and picks Kids or Teens from the child's age. Creating the profile is the parent's consent.
    • ApprovalSwitching on, changing or removing a child's profile needs the owner's biometric, even on the child's own phone.
    • PaymentKids and Teens profiles are free and never show ads.

    Guardians

    Parent is no longer a variant. Guardian is a role that any adult variant can hold, so a salaried parent, a homemaker and a teacher can all be guardians of the same child.

    • Adding guardiansA parent sets up the child's account and is the first guardian. The parent can add a teacher or other trusted adults, and remove them at any time. The child can see who their guardians are.
    • MessagesThe child can message their guardians and nobody else. Parents can see these chats, and every chat has a report button. A kid is never handed off to WhatsApp, Telegram or the phone: calls and messages stay inside the app.
    • TeachersA verified teacher sees their class list and can message only the children whose parents added them.

    Me, the 13th app

    Me holds who the user is. The other 12 apps read from it, so nothing is entered twice.

    • HoldsProfile and username, the lists of people, child control, plus the variant, language, location, birthday and subscription.
    • FamilyGuardian links and child control live here. Messages and shared Reminders use them.
    • Lists of peopleSeparate lists, shown below. Messages, Meetings, Hobbies and Events read from them.
    • Reach meDecided: one optional mobile number and one app for messages: WhatsApp, Telegram or SMS. The number is not the account and is never checked by OTP. It is used only to open a chat or a call for people in the lists. A guardian fills it for a teen. Kids have none.
    • Calls and messagesOne-to-one talk is handed off by a plain link, with no APIs and no fees: the phone's dialer for a call, and WhatsApp, Telegram or SMS for a message, whichever that person chose. WhatsApp has no link that starts a call. Groups, such as the class chat, stay inside the app. This holds for Messages and Meetings in every adult variant until in-app calls are built.
    • Work detailsSkills feed Earn, and a business card feeds Yellow Pages and Advertise.

    Kinds of person

    A person can hold more than one variant. A kind of person is one allowed combination of variants. With the rules below there are 97 kinds: 11 with one variant, 31 with two and 55 with three.

    • ChildrenKids and Teens always stand alone. They are profiles an adult makes and never mix with another variant. That is 2 kinds.
    • At most threeAn adult holds one, two or three of the nine adult variants, so a Home tile opens into at most three versions.
    • Pairs that clashLooking for a job with Office job. Retired with Office job. Retired with Looking for a job. Teacher with Office job, because teaching is the job. Student with Retired.
    • Everything else mixesFor example Farming with Shop, Home with Daily work, Teacher with Farming, Retired with Shop.
    • Without the limit of threeThe same clashes alone would give 193 kinds. With no rules at all there would be 2,047.

    News, pushed

    News is called Khabri in every variant. It is the one name that binds all the variants together. News comes to the user without being asked for. It builds on the existing Khabri project. The aim is that a person gets the good, latest content here and no longer needs to dig for it in social networks.

    • More than newsEach variant's feed mixes its news with the best new things for that age group, such as hit music and videos. This is added to the news topics already listed for each variant.
    • Kids NewsChildren get news made for children, plus songs, videos and the latest kids content.
    • Playing mediaVideos play in the embedded YouTube player. YouTube Kids is a separate app, so for Kids we embed normal YouTube videos that are marked made for kids and hand-picked, with tracking turned off as YouTube requires. Nothing is behind a paywall, which respects YouTube's rule against charging to watch an embedded video. Adult variants also get music in the embedded Spotify player, alongside YouTube. Kids and Teens get YouTube only.
    • How a khabar is made1. Topics arrive from the chosen X handles. 2. Khabri picks the ones worth telling. 3. It gathers the facts from permitted sources. 4. It writes the khabar from scratch, in four levels, so a bright reader of any age finds a level that suits them. 5. It produces the khabar in the 12 languages, as text and audio. 6. It pushes the khabar to the variants it fits. Khabri's existing pipeline is reused for steps 2 to 4.
    • FeedOne khabar at a time on a card, as in Khabri today. A swipe says like or not for me, and the feed learns from it. The variant sets the starting topics.
    • From KhabriAlready built: the card deck, taste learning, four reading levels, a pipeline that finds and writes khabars every half hour, and Dodo payments for ads.
    • To addKhabri writes in English only, learns taste without an account, and carries only positive news. BodhiLabs needs 12 languages, taste tied to the account and variant, and practical items such as mandi prices and job alerts.
    • SourcesIndian news, plus global nature, science and health. No site crawling and no paid news API. Topics come from X handles. Facts come from sources that permit reuse: government press releases, open government data and Wikipedia. Khabri's pipeline will be limited to these.
    • X handlesKhabri follows about 20 chosen handles: a handful of Indian news outlets, plus global nature, science and health accounts. It reads their posts through X's paid API, with a cap of 10,000 posts or about 50 US dollars a month, and no scraping. A post is only a pointer to a topic. X's terms do not allow rewriting, translating or summarising the post itself.
    • Spotify rulesThe Spotify player is shown unaltered, with no ad on or over it, and never in Kids or Teens.
    • TrustEvery khabar names its source and its date.

    Wisdom, quotations as stories

    Wisdom is the top centre tile on Home, right above Learning, and has the same name in every variant. It is designed now and built last. Its screens are on the shell canvas.

    • What it holdsA list of quotations, one new each day. Each is shown word for word with its source.
    • The storyUnder each quotation is a short moral science story, ending in one line of moral. It can be read or heard.
    • Per variantThe same quotation has a different story for each variant, set in that person's own life. A person with several variants can change between their stories.
    • Kept simpleA heart keeps a quotation. There are no ads, no comments and no scores.
    • ScaleNothing is written per person. Each story is made once for a variant and a language, checked, and served as a saved file.
    • Not decidedWhich traditions are covered, where the quotations come from and who checks them. These are in the Wisdom backlog.

    Modifiers

    These do not create new variants. They apply to whichever variant the user is in. Onboarding asks only for a language, a PIN code and a variant, in that order.

    Language

    • The first onboarding screen shows 12 blocks, one per language, each written in its own script. The user taps one.
    • The 12 are Hindi, English, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Urdu, Kannada, Odia, Malayalam and Punjabi.
    • The choice sets the UI, the voice and the news language.
    • It can be changed later in Me.

    Location

    • Taken once as a PIN code, or from the phone's location with permission. The user never sees a city tier.
    • It changes content only: listings, events, mandi prices and local news.
    • Where listings are thin, nearby widens to the nearest town.
    • Low data and offline modes switch on where the network is weak.

    Voice first, for everyone

    • This is a rule for the whole app and not a setting. Nobody is asked whether they can read.
    • Every screen can be heard, and every input can be spoken.
    • Reading and typing are optional and stay one tap away, for quiet or public places.
    • Icons, pictures and numbers carry meaning so a screen works without reading.

    One backend, many variants

    Decided: the backend is the same for every variant. A variant is a set of settings on top of it.

    • SharedThe 13 apps, their data, the news store, accounts, guardian links and voice pipeline are built once and serve everyone.
    • Per variantApp names, icons and theme, tone of prompts, default topics and sources, which apps are on, and safety rules. An app that is off for a variant is hidden, not shown locked.
    • SafetyRules for Kids and Teens are checked by the backend on every request, so they hold even if the screen is bypassed.
    • New variantAdding one later, as happened with Teachers, means writing a new settings file and not new code.

    Free with ads, or subscribe

    Decided: adults can use every app without paying. Free use is paid for by ads. A subscription of 1,200 rupees a year gives the option to remove them.

    • FreeAll 13 apps, with ads. The ads come from BodhiLabs's own Advertise app, as Khabri sells them today.
    • SubscribedThe same 13 apps, with the option to turn ads off.
    • Kids and TeensFree, and never shown ads.
    • Where ads goNever in News, never in Wisdom, and never on or over a YouTube or Spotify player.

    Install and first run

    BodhiLabs is a PWA. The steps below are the agreed flow.

    • WebsiteOpening the site shows a landing page that explains the app. The only action on it is Install.
    • InstallInstall adds the PWA to the device. On Android and desktop Chrome the button can open the install prompt. On iPhone the browser gives no install prompt, so the page has to show the Share then Add to Home Screen steps.
    • OnboardingThe first screen is 12 language blocks. Then comes the PIN code. Then one screen of 9 picture blocks, where an adult picks their variant.
    • First openThe app asks the user to set up biometrics before anything else. In a PWA this is a passkey, unlocked by fingerprint or face. On a phone with no sensor, the device PIN is the fallback.
    • SubscriptionOptional. All apps are free with ads. Removing ads costs 1,200 rupees a year, paid by scanning a UPI QR code through Dodo Payments.
    • Payment feesDodo's listed India rate is 4% plus 15 US cents, with 0.5% more for subscriptions. On 1,200 rupees a year that is about 67 rupees, assuming 85 rupees to the dollar. Recurring UPI charges confirm about 48 hours after the charge date.
    • Age checkNone for now. Child profiles can only be created by an adult owner.
    • Variant changeA person can hold any number of variants, for example a farmer who also runs a shop. Home shows one tile per app. Where the person's variants call an app by different names, the tile carries the app's own name and opens into those versions, for example Earnings opens into Mandi and Leads. A long press lets the person keep a tile on top, move it or hide it. Adding or removing a variant asks for biometrics again. For a child's profile it must be the owner's biometric, so a child cannot leave Kids or Teens on their own.
