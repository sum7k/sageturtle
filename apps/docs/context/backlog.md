## Admin dashboard (first version built 2026-11-28; sumeet asked for the basics only)

- A chart of people per day, and of each funnel step per day.
- Coming back: how many people return the next day, after 7 days, after 30 days.
- Gyan: which words and letters are got wrong most, how many rounds a person does, time spent.
- The funnel split by language, by state and by who sent the person.
- Voices: how many recordings are made and played, and ElevenLabs' cost so far.
- City or district, not only state.
- Download the numbers as a file.
- A morning summary sent to sumeet (WhatsApp or email).
- Alerts: no new people for a day, errors on the server, a failed deploy.
- More than one admin, each with their own sign-in (Cloudflare Access already works for this).
- Controls, not only numbers: open or lock an app, change the picture pool, without a deploy.
- Counting visits and installs is open to anyone inflating it; if that happens, count through Cloudflare's own analytics instead.
