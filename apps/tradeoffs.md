# BodhiLabs apps: tradeoffs and choices

Each line is a choice made without asking, or a risk accepted. It gives the choice, the reason, and what we gave up. Only the current choice is kept; a line is replaced when the choice changes. Newest at the bottom.

Format: `- [where] Choice. Why. Cost / what we gave up.`

- [teens] Teens see no ads, same as Kids. Why: Indian data law bars targeted ads to under 18s, and untargeted ads earn little. Cost: no ad income from Teens profiles.
- [onboarding] An adult picks their variant on one onboarding screen of 9 picture blocks, after the language and PIN code screens. Why: it matches the 12 language blocks and needs no reading. Cost: a person who fits two variants must pick one.
- [apps] An app that is off for a variant is hidden, not shown locked. Why: fewer things on a voice-first screen. Cost: a user does not learn that the app exists.
- [names] The app names for each variant are working titles in English. Why: the map needed names to be readable. Cost: none are checked with users or translated. News is the exception: you named it Khabri in every variant on 2026-11-26.
- [names] Human: every app name is one word and a noun, never a verb (2026-11-26). Why: one word fits a tile; verbs bring an agitation tied to time. Cost: some names are less direct, for example Listings instead of Sell.
- [location] City tier is replaced by a PIN code, which only filters content. Why: a tier cannot be asked of a user. Cost: none found. You did not confirm this in words.
- [news] No paid news API. Why: I compared about 20 news APIs on 2026-11-26. They cost 19 to 450 US dollars a month and none grants the right to republish full articles. Cost: no ready feed of Indian news. Every khabar is our own writing.
- [news] Topics come from about 20 X handles through X's paid API, capped at 10,000 posts or about 50 US dollars a month (you approved this). A post is only a pointer to a topic. Khabri never rewrites, translates or summarises the post. Why: X's Developer Agreement lets us "Modify X Content only to format it for display" and bars derivative works and scraping. Cost: a topic seen only on X, with no permitted source for its facts, is skipped. Human: when you open the X developer account, state this use case exactly, since X treats it as binding, and ask if a monetised app needs the Enterprise plan. I could not read X's Display Requirements page.
- [news] Facts for a khabar come only from sources that permit reuse: government press releases, open government data and Wikipedia. Khabri's pipeline will stop reading other publisher pages. Why: your rule is "we will not crawl sites". Cost: fewer khabars, and the Khabri code needs this change.
- [news] Khabri's existing pick-and-write pipeline and its four levels (Novice to Advanced) are reused. The four levels are how one khabar serves bright readers of different ages. Why: it is built and you like it. Cost: Khabri writes in English only today, so the 12 languages are new work.
- [youtube] For Kids we embed normal YouTube videos that are marked "made for kids", hand-picked, with tracking off. Why: YouTube Kids has no embeddable player, and YouTube requires tracking off for such videos. Cost: someone must pick the videos.
- [spotify] Spotify is for adult variants only. The player is shown unaltered, with no ad on or over it. Why: its widget terms allow embedding on sites that carry ads, forbid covering it, and forbid it on services targeted to children. Our News page is free and has no ads. Cost: one small risk stays. The terms bar "integrating" Spotify with streams from another service, and YouTube is in the same feed. I read two separate players on separate cards as fine.
- [payments] Dodo Payments' listed India rate is 4% plus 15 US cents, plus 0.5% for subscriptions. That is about 67 rupees on 1,200 rupees a year. Recurring UPI charges confirm about 48 hours after the charge date. Cost: renewals need a short grace period.
- [design] All 13 apps take Khabri's theme, and each app gets its own soft tint. Why: you liked the theme, it needs no photos, and its Anek font covers nine Indian scripts. Cost: Khabri has no night colours, so I made new ones. Urdu needs a separate font. Which tint goes to which app is my first guess.
- [variants] Human: a person can hold a variable number of variants (2026-11-26). Adding or removing one asks for the lock; lists of people are the union. Why: a farmer who runs a shop is both. Cost: more states to design and test.
- [variants] Nobody holds more than three variants. Why: a Home tile then opens into at most three versions, and more is rare. Cost: a person with four real roles must leave one out.
- [variants] Five pairs clash: Looking for a job with Office job; Retired with Office job; Retired with Looking for a job; Teacher with Office job; Student with Retired. Kids and Teens stand alone. Why: each pair says two opposite things about the same part of life. Cost: a retired person who wants a job, or a working person hunting for a new one, must pick one.
- [variants] With these rules there are 97 kinds of person: 11 with one variant, 31 with two, 55 with three. Without the limit of three it would be 193; with no rules 2,047. Why: a number small enough to test every kind. Cost: none.
- [home] Human approved 2026-11-26: Home shows one tile per app. Where the person's variants call an app by different names, the tile carries the app's own name and opens into those versions (Earnings opens into Mandi and Leads). With one name it opens straight away. Why: the same app is grouped, yet each variant keeps its own name. Cost: a person with several variants needs one more tap, and sees generic names on the closed tiles.
- [home] Group tiles use these names: Learning, Earnings, Reminders, Hobbies, Meetings, Messages, Discussions, Events, Directory, Adverts. Why: one word, a noun, close to what the app is. Cost: not checked with users.
- [home] A long press arranges a tile: keep on top, move earlier or later, hide. Hidden tiles wait behind a last grey tile. No folders. Why: the human asked for favourites, archive and reorder; grouping by app already does the job of folders. Cost: moving is by arrow buttons, not by dragging, in the first build.
- [launch] Human, 2026-11-28: renamed to BodhiLabs; apps released one by one; Home has 11 buttons with placeholder icons, the first row open (Book, Horoscope, Khabri) and eight locked that count taps; the top of Home is the ad spot, for now a question that opens a form; registration required; admin at bodhilabs.org/admin; Cloudflare only; words in 11 languages and no voice.
- [launch] The eight locked buttons are Earnings, Reminders, Hobbies, Meetings, Messages, Discussions, Events and Directory, and each shows its name. Why: a tap on a named button tells us which app people want; a tap on a nameless icon tells us only which picture they like. Adverts is left out because Home already has the ad spot, and Wisdom because it is built last. Cost: the names themselves steer the taps; the human said "random icons" and may have wanted no names.
- [launch] The 11 languages are the earlier twelve without Punjabi. Why: the human said 11; Punjabi has the fewest speakers of the twelve (2011 census). Cost: about 3 crore Punjabi speakers get Hindi or English.
- [launch] The ten Indian languages are written by Claude. Why: to launch with words everywhere. Cost: not checked by native speakers; some words will be stiff or wrong.
- [launch] Home ignores the person's variants for now: everyone sees the same 11 buttons. Why: tap counts must compare the same buttons across people. Cost: the variant names and grouped tiles wait in the design. Variants are still asked at registration, so taps can later be split by work.
- [analytics] Taps are kept in Cloudflare D1, one row per person, button and day, not one row per tap. Why: distinct users is a plain count, the table grows with people and not with taps, and the admin page needs no outside tool. Cost: we do not keep the time of each tap. At very large scale (crores of users) taps should move to Workers Analytics Engine; D1 holds 10 GB per database.
- [analytics] The server is told only language, PIN code and work, under a random id. No name, no number. Why: enough to count and to group; nothing personal to leak. Cost: we cannot contact a user, and a person on two phones counts twice.
- [analytics] Taps wait on the phone and are sent in one message. Why: nothing is lost without network, and fewer requests. Cost: a count can arrive a little late.
- [analytics] Nothing stops fake registrations yet. Why: no abuse yet, and a check adds friction. Cost: the numbers can be inflated by a script; add Cloudflare Turnstile when it happens.
- [admin] The admin page is protected twice: Cloudflare Access in front, and the Worker checks Access's signature itself. Why: the page stays shut even if Access is switched off by mistake. Cost: two settings to fill in before the page opens.
- [hosting] The pages are static files; one Worker answers only /api and /admin. Why: page views cost nothing and scale without limit; only taps run code. Cost: no server-side rendering.
- [book] Book v1 is the letters of the person's language; a tap shows the letter large and the phone's own voice says it. Why: a letter cannot be taught without its sound, the phone's voice costs nothing, and the human allowed media where it adds value. Cost: some phones have no voice for some languages; there are no words, pictures or lessons yet.
- [horoscope] Horoscope v1 is a sign and one line a day from seven hand-written lines of gentle advice, in English and Hindi. Why: a real horoscope needs a content decision by the human; advice cannot mislead. Cost: it is not astrology, and nine languages see English lines.
- [cleanup] The old placeholder landing page (`software/`) moved to `.trash/software` in the repository. Why: the app itself now serves the domain. Cost: none; it was never deployed.
