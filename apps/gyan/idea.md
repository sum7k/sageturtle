# Gyan: the idea

sumeet, 2026-11-28. Gyan is the first tab on Home. It was called Book, then Study; sumeet said "study is something
else, this is gyan".

- The subjects are not separate. It is one thing: the growth of the human being, from the past to the present. When
  people learned to light a fire, the learner learns fire; when they drew on a wall, the learner draws; when song and
  dance came, song and dance; when music came, music. Everything is in order from the start.
- A topic arrives at its time, not at its school class. If zero had not come by the time of Pythagoras, then it has not come.
- Drawing (the art of seeing: line, perspective), music and exercise are part of it. Exercise holds martial arts and
  dance. Photography and the performing arts can be taught. For things done by hand, such as pottery, the app shows
  how and trusts the learner to do it. Nothing is recorded or shared: this is not a social network.
- The app does not know the learner's level, so it tests: a picture is shown and the learner picks the right word.
  About three such tests clear a stage.
- There are 12 stages (the number 11 was dropped; everything is 12 now). Clearing all of them equals class 11, taught
  so well that a learner is ready for IIT (JEE) and NEET. Class 12 in school is mostly preparation for those tests.
  NCERT is the reference for what school teaches in which class. The learner need not take years: one year can be enough.
- Learners differ. Someone weak in one thing, such as maths, should still move ahead.
- Three tabs: Gyan (the past up to today), Vartaman (the present; this is Khabri renamed) and Bhavishya (the future;
  the horoscope, designed later). Vartaman opens when the learner has finished the level of class 6, and Bhavishya after it.
- The same content is given in all 11 languages.
