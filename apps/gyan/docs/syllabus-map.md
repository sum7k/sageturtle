# Gyan: the path

Written 2026-11-28. This is a document only; nothing here is built. Gyan is the first tab on Home (it was called
Book, then Study).

## 1. What this is

Gyan is one path, not a set of subjects. It is the growth of the human being, told from the start and walked by the
learner in the same order: the first lines on a cave wall, fire, song, the pot, the wheel, writing, the right triangle,
zero, the telescope, the vote. When people learned to count, the learner counts. When people learned to paint, the
learner paints. It ends in the present day.

- **One path.** There are no subjects to choose. Each step carries a small label (Number, Nature, People, Earth, Rule,
  Money, Word, Art, Music, Body, Hand) only so that we can see what the path holds.
- **Order of time.** A step arrives when the world reached it.
- **One world timeline, seen from India.** An idea enters when people first found it, anywhere on Earth. Where India
  was first, India's story is told and its name is used: the rule of the right triangle is Baudhayana's rule (about
  800 BCE), with Pythagoras named after him; zero is Brahmagupta's (628 CE). So the triangle comes before zero in
  India too.
- **Two kinds of step.** A *know* step is read and understood, and it can be tested. A *do* step shows how a thing is
  done (a pot, a drawing, a song, an exercise) and trusts the learner to do it; it is never tested, recorded or shared.
- **12 stages.** Each stage is an age of the human story. Clearing all 12 gives what school teaches up to class 11,
  with the science and maths deep enough for the JEE and NEET entrance tests. The NCERT class is written beside each
  step that school also teaches.
- **Not one stage a year.** A keen learner can walk the whole path in about a year.
- The content is the same in every language of the app.

## 3. How a learner moves (the machine)

There are 13 states: stages 1 to 12 and Done. A learner is at exactly one stage and only moves forward.

```
        pass entry test                    pass entry test
      (already knows it)                 (already knows it)
   ┌──────────────────────┐           ┌──────────────────────┐
   │                      ▼           │                      ▼
[Stage 1] ─── steps ─── exit test ─▶ [Stage 2] ─── steps ─── exit test ─▶ ... ─▶ [Stage 12] ─▶ [Done]
              ▲            │
              └── not yet ─┘
```

**Entry test.** The quick check at the door of a stage: does the learner already know it? It has 3 rounds. All 3
passed: the stage is cleared and the next entry test begins. Any round missed: the steps of this stage start.

- In stages 1 and 2 nothing is read. A round is 5 questions of the kind "hear the word, tap the right picture" (the
  app says "pigeon"; three pictures are shown), and 4 right passes it.
- In stage 3 a round includes the written word: a pigeon is shown, and the learner picks "pigeon" from three written words.
- From stage 4 a round is 5 read questions mixed from the stage's know steps, and 4 right passes it.

**Exit test.** After the steps: 10 questions mixed from all the know steps of the stage. 7 right passes. The mix means
a learner who is weak on one side, such as numbers, is not held back by it. There is no limit on tries.

**Placement.** The app does not know the learner's level. Everyone starts at the entry test of stage 1 and climbs.
The first entry test missed is where the learner begins.

**Rules.**
- Do steps are shown, not tested. The learner taps "done" in their own time. Nothing is recorded and nothing is shared.
- No marks or ranks are shown. A stage is either cleared or not.
- A cleared stage stays cleared, and its steps stay open.
- Clearing stage 7 opens Vartaman, then Bhavishya. Clearing stage 12 completes Gyan: equal to class 11, and ready for JEE and NEET.

**Reading.** People spoke, sang and drew for tens of thousands of years before anyone wrote, and the learner does the
same. Stages 1 and 2 have no reading at all: every step is a picture and a spoken word. Letters are taught in stage 3,
where writing was invented. From stage 4 the steps are read (section 5).

## 4A. Strands that run through all 12 stages

sumeet, 2026-11-28: some things are not taught well in school but are worth knowing. They are not new subjects. Each
is a strand inside the existing labels, present in every stage, light at first and deeper later, and placed by time
like everything else. The steps of section 4 stay; these add to them.

Three rules:

- **Tradition and science are both taught, and kept apart.** Jyotish, vastu and Ayurveda are taught as traditions:
  what they say, how they work, where they came from. Astronomy, building science and medicine are taught as science:
  what has been tested. A step always says which of the two it is. Ayurveda is never offered in place of a doctor.
- **Religions are told with respect, each in its own words:** how it arose, its teachers and books, what it teaches,
  how its people live and celebrate. No religion is compared with or ranked against another.
- **Most of these are do steps:** a breath, a song, a dish, a knot, an argument. They are shown and trusted, not tested.

## 8. What the order of time costs

- **Zero comes late.** Until stage 6 numbers are written without zero, as counts of ones, tens and hundreds, and sums
  are done with counters. School arithmetic (class 2 to 5) is taught at stage 6.
- **The class jumps about.** Stage 4 holds class 8 work (the right triangle) before stage 6 holds class 2 work (written
  sums). The class says what school calls a step, not how hard it is.
- **Reading comes at stage 3.** Stages 1 and 2 need a picture and a voice for every step. A person who can already
  read still begins at stage 1, and skips ahead by the entry tests.
- **Do steps rest on trust.** The app shows; it cannot see whether the pot was made or the song was sung.
- **Stages 8 to 11 are very heavy.** Almost all of class 11 and 12 physics, chemistry and maths was found between
  1600 and 1947, so the JEE and NEET work sits there, not in stage 12.
- **JEE and NEET need class 12 too.** Their official syllabus is NCERT class 11 and class 12 together. To make a
  learner ready, the path carries the class 12 science and maths as well; those steps are marked 12.
- **Only science and maths go to class 11 and beyond.** History, geography, civics and economics stay at class 10
  level, because class 11 splits into streams there.
